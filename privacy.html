We use this information to respond to appointment requests, coordinate bookings, and maintain appointment records.

We retain information only as needed for appointment management and legitimate operational purposes. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact the service operator using the contact details provided through the service.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the operator of Appointment Booking Agent through the contact details provided with the service.
